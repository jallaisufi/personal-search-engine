Tax return checklist (2026 tax year)

Documents to collect:
- Annual income statement from the employer (Lohnsteuerbescheinigung)
- Receipts for work equipment: laptop, monitor, desk chair
- Home office days: keep a simple list of dates worked from home
- Travel costs to work: distance and number of days in the office
- Health insurance and pension contribution statements
- Donation receipts

Submission:
Without a tax advisor, the 2026 tax return must be submitted by 31 July 2027.
Submit electronically via ELSTER. Keep all receipts for at least ten years.

Notes:
Professional development costs, such as online courses and technical books, are deductible as work-related expenses. The new laptop bought in October counts as work equipment and can be fully deducted in the year of purchase.
