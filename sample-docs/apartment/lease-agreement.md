# Residential Lease Agreement

Apartment 4B, Lindenstraße 12, 80331 Munich. Landlord: Hausverwaltung Berger GmbH. Tenant: Alex Example.

## Rent and Payments

The monthly base rent is 1,150 EUR plus 220 EUR in advance payments for heating and operating costs. Rent is due on the third working day of each month by bank transfer.

The first rent payment is due on 1 December 2026.

## Security Deposit

The tenant pays a security deposit of three months' base rent (3,450 EUR). It may be paid in three equal monthly instalments, the first together with the first rent payment. The deposit is returned within six months after the lease ends, minus any justified claims.

## Rent Increases

The landlord may raise the rent no more than once every fifteen months and only up to the local comparative rent (Mietspiegel). Any increase must be requested in writing with a justification. The tenant has until the end of the second month after receiving the request to agree.

## Notice Period

The tenant can terminate the lease with three months' notice, in writing, by the third working day of a month. The landlord's notice period depends on the length of the tenancy.

## Pets and Subletting

Small animals such as hamsters or fish are allowed. Cats and dogs require written consent. Subletting part of the apartment requires the landlord's written permission.
